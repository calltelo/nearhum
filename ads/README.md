## The pitch, in one line

Nearhum is a hyperlocal, voice-only social feed — you drop a voice post, people nearby hear it and reply in their own voice. No text, no DMs, no calls.

## How it works (shared explainer — reuse this everywhere)

1. **Sign up** — email + password, pick a handle. Takes under a minute.
2. **Share your area** — Nearhum uses your rough location (never an exact pin) to find voices near you: your block, your city, or your whole region.
3. **Listen** — the feed auto-plays like a radio tuned to your neighborhood. Nearest and freshest voices play first.
4. **Reply in your voice** — no typing, no text replies. You hold to record and hum back.
5. **It fades** — every post disappears after 24 hours, so the feed always sounds like *right now*.
6. **React light** — three taps: felt it, same, or loud. No comments to write.
7. **New accounts get free credits** — enough plays and drops to try the whole loop before spending anything.
